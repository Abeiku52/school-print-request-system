User Guide

Learn how to use the Print Request System effectively

Quick Start

1

Submit a Print Request

Click "New Request" to upload your document and specify print settings.

2

Track Your Request

Monitor progress on your dashboard. You'll receive notifications for status updates.

3

Collect Your Prints

When completed, collect your documents from the print center.

Key Features

Reprint Documents

Easily reprint completed requests without re-uploading files. Just click the "Reprint" button on any completed request.

Real-time Notifications

Get instant updates when your request status changes. Check the bell icon for new notifications.

Easy Search

Find your requests quickly using the search bar. Search by request number, file name, or status.

How to Reprint Documents

1. Find Your Completed Request

Go to your dashboard and look for requests with "Completed" status. These will show a blue "Reprint" button.

2. Click Reprint

Click the "Reprint" button on any completed request. This will open a form pre-filled with your original settings.

3. Modify Settings (Optional)

Adjust the number of copies, paper size, color options, or finishing preferences as needed.

4. Submit

Click "Create Reprint Request" to submit. You'll get a new request number and can track it separately.

Tips & Best Practices

Check your credit balance before submitting large print jobs.

Use page ranges to print only specific pages (e.g., "1-5, 8, 10-15").

Enable notifications in your browser to get instant updates.

Keep request numbers for easy reference when collecting prints.

Use reprints to save time when you need additional copies.

Request Status Guide

Pending

Your request is waiting to be reviewed by administrators.

In Progress

Your request is being processed and printed.

Completed

Your prints are ready for collection. You can also reprint from here.

Cancelled

The request was cancelled (by you or an administrator).
